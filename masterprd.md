Executive Summary
BizTrack Lite is a simple, mobile-first business record-keeping application designed specifically for nano, micro, small, medium, and growing enterprises. It enables business owners to easily record inventory, purchases, sales, expenses, revenue, profits, and customer transactions without requiring accounting knowledge.
Unlike traditional accounting software that can be overwhelming, BizTrack Lite focuses on simplicity. It is designed for everyone, including market women, roadside traders, artisans, farmers, shop owners, and entrepreneurs with limited literacy. The application works both online and offline, ensuring uninterrupted use even in areas with unreliable internet access.
The product aims to improve financial discipline, help businesses make informed decisions, increase access to financing, and support long-term business growth through accurate record keeping.

Vision Statement
To empower every entrepreneur, regardless of education level or business size, with a simple and affordable digital tool for managing business records, improving profitability, and building financially sustainable enterprises.

Mission Statement
To eliminate poor record keeping among small businesses by providing an intuitive financial management platform that anyone can use within minutes.

Problem Statement
Across many developing economies, especially Nigeria and other African countries, a significant number of small businesses still rely on memory or paper notebooks to manage their finances. This creates several challenges:
Poor inventory management
Inability to determine daily profit
Difficulty tracking expenses
Loss of business records
Poor financial decisions
Difficulty obtaining loans due to lack of financial records
Limited understanding of business performance
Existing accounting software often targets trained accountants and is too expensive or complex for small businesses.
BizTrack Lite bridges this gap by offering an easy-to-use financial record system tailored to everyday entrepreneurs.

Competitive Advantage
BizTrack Lite is designed around the realities of African small businesses. Rather than adapting complex accounting software, it starts with the daily habits of market traders and small entrepreneurs. The interface emphasizes large icons, minimal text, local language options, offline capability, and voice-assisted data entry, making it accessible to users with limited literacy. At the same time, it offers a clear growth path with advanced features for businesses as they expand.
Product Vision Statement
BizTrack Lite exists to ensure that no entrepreneur is excluded from sound financial management because of limited education, limited technology access, or the complexity of traditional accounting software. By making record keeping simple, intuitive, and available anywhere, it empowers millions of businesses to understand their finances, improve profitability, and build the financial credibility needed to grow, access funding, and create lasting economic impact.
